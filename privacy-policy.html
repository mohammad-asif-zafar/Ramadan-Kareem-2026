Privacy Policy

Noor Muslim Companion

Last updated: February 12, 2027

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Noor Muslim Companion handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Location Information

The app may access approximate or precise location to calculate accurate prayer times and display nearby mosques. This location data is used only on your device and is not stored, shared, or sold to external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or payment data.

2. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services to provide core features:

  • AlQuran Cloud API: Provides Quranic text and audio streams.
  • Adhan Library (Batoul Apps): Used for offline prayer time calculations.
  • Google Maps & Places APIs: Powers the mosque discovery and map features.
  • Google Play Services: Manages location updates and basic app infrastructure.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used strictly to provide app functionality. For example:

  • Your GPS coordinates are used to calculate the Qibla direction.
  • Your location is used to determine the correct prayer time calculation method for your region.

The app does not use your data for advertising, user profiling, or tracking.

4. Data Storage & Security

The app does not store personal data on external servers. All network communication (e.g., fetching Quran data) is performed over secure HTTPS connections.

5. Permissions

To function correctly, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Location: Required for accurate prayer times and finding nearby mosques.
  • Internet: Required to fetch Quran content and map data.
  • Notifications: Required to play Azan alerts and prayer reminders.
  • Vibration: Used for notification haptics.

6. Children’s Privacy

Noor Muslim Companion does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
